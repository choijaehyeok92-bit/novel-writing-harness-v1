## Evidence caution

- These are strong specialist secondary syntheses, not direct editions of every underlying primary document.
- Claims about the precise employer, number, organization or legal status of German mercenaries in **1198** are not directly established by these sources.
- The story's pre-1198 Constantinople **bailo** is an intentional treaty-based alternate-history divergence and should be evaluated from canon, not corrected from this source layer.
