# World Bible Overrides — Chapter 113

> This file supersedes chapter-112-era statements in `canon/world-bible.md` where chapter 113 has now enacted previously planned military institutions. For all other topics, `canon/world-bible.md` remains authoritative together with later story/timeline/arc canon.

## Military professionalization — UPDATED THROUGH CH113

- The **Polemiki Scholi** and **Taxis ton Strategon** remain established as in chapter 112.
- Chapter 113 now establishes that senior command-and-staff education is operating in practice, including Roman military history, war/politics theory, administration, taxation/mobilization, military law, war finance, logistics, intelligence analysis and high command.
- The senior course uses a four-month continuous evolving hypothetical war and final specialist/red-blue exercises. Alexios Gidos and Kaloyan are shown learning through opposed operational approaches and cross-adaptation.
- The **Sekreton tou Stratopedou / 세크레톤 투 스트라토페두** is now an established field-headquarters framework, no longer merely a future staff concept. Its six functions are personnel/rolls, intelligence, operations, logistics, engineering/weapons, and documents/communications.
- Staff do not supersede command authority: the commander decides; staff officers support decision, planning, coordination and execution.
- The system is still an embryonic Roman staff structure, **not** a fully mature modern General Staff or numbered G-staff.

## Standardized field-force structure — ESTABLISHED CURRENT TARGETS

- Smallest group: roughly 10–12.
- Intermediate groupings: roughly 50–60 and 100–120.
- **Allagion:** roughly 300–400.
- **Tagma:** roughly 900–1,200 and primarily branch-centered.
- **Megas tagma:** roughly 3,000–4,500, assembled from several tagmata/supporting arms as a combined-arms field grouping.
- **Stratopedon:** roughly 8,000–15,000, an operational army grouping formed from multiple megala tagmata and support.
- Current reformed standing central force: about **8 tagmata / roughly 10,000 men**.
- Forecast only, not achieved: about **15–18 tagmata / roughly 20,000 standing troops around 1200** if finances permit.

## Officer qualification — STAGED POLICY

- For four years Polemiki Scholi graduates receive promotion advantage rather than exclusive access.
- Planned later rule: tagma deputy-command level and above should normally complete the academy regular or serving-officer special course.
- Planned from 1205: megas-tagma-level commanders and imperial direct operations staff should normally complete **Taxis ton Strategon**.
- These future thresholds are policy intentions, not yet realized outcomes.

## Western strategic status — UPDATED THROUGH CH113

- Henry VI is now explicitly **Imperator**; chapter 113 confirms an imperial coronation has occurred after the earlier Romanorum-Rex phase, but gives no exact date.
- Frederick VI of Swabia is now actually **confined** by Henry despite Henry's explicit acknowledgment of his innocence.
- Henry orders the Sicilian invasion in Constance's name; Alexios orders Sicily warned to prepare. The war has begun but its outcome is not yet established.
