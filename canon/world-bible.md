# World Bible

> Canon state through manuscript chapter 107. `ESTABLISHED` means enacted/on-page. `PLANNED` means Alexios has discussed or designed it but the manuscript has not yet established full implementation.

## Political system

### Imperial authority — ESTABLISHED
- Alexios II remains an autocratic Roman emperor with enormous personal prestige, but deliberately turns portions of imperial discretion into written procedure and delegated institutions.
- Major legitimating pillars by 1192: law of succession, Synkletos/Syllogos approval, patriarchal coronation, and army/citizen proclamation.
- Alexios's political method is not modern democracy: institutions operate inside an explicitly monarchical Roman order and depend heavily on the prestige and coercive capacity of the reigning emperor.

### Reformed Synkletos — ESTABLISHED
- Fixed **100 lifetime seats**:
  - 30 appointed directly by the emperor.
  - 30 selected by Constantinopolitan citizens.
  - 15 judges recommended through the high judiciary.
  - 15 appointed on recommendation of the Ecumenical Patriarch.
  - 10 appointed on recommendation of the University of Constantinople.
- Reviews and votes on petitions arriving from the Syllogos.
- A rejected petition normally returns to Syllogos for reconsideration.
- Syllogos measures passed by two-thirds, and tax-related measures, go directly to the emperor rather than being stopped by the Synkletos.

### Syllogos — ESTABLISHED
- First election: **1187-11**. First national sitting: **spring 1188**.
- **166 seats** in the first apportionment, allocated among four major cities and themes broadly by population, with minimum theme representation and six emperor-nominated seats representing unrecovered territory.
- Members are chosen by local civic/theme assemblies combining urban councils, guild/community interests, church/parish representation, military/tax communities, and local administration.
- Term: **4 years** in the first established design.
- Political groupings emerge as:
  - **Chrysozonoi / 금대당**: broadly pro-expansion, dynatoi/merchant and strong-war interests.
  - **Kyanophoroi / 청의당**: more provincial/fiscal-burden conscious, including Asen's circle.
- Party organization is permitted but registration, annual double-entry accounts, donor disclosure, and anti-bribery/anti-patronage rules are imposed.
- Secret ballot is used for at least key internal elections; John Doukas becomes the first presiding figure after a contested vote.

### Imperial succession law — ESTABLISHED (1192)
- Article 1: the throne is entrusted by God to the Romans; the emperor is its trustee, not private owner.
- Article 3: one who seizes the throne by force is not emperor; following him is treason; his descendants are excluded.
- Succession ordinarily follows a co-emperor designated by the reigning emperor.
- Preference order: eldest purple-born son; other sons; daughter and spouse; emperor's brothers and their sons; male-line close kin of imperial blood.
- If no valid designation exists, Synkletos and Syllogos jointly elevate the highest eligible person in that order; only if none is suitable may they go outside Komnenian blood.
- Eligibility: Orthodox, adult (or governed by regency rule), mentally capable, and not convicted of treason/parricide.
- For a minor emperor, the Augusta acts as regent with delegates from Synkletos and Syllogos; regency ends at majority.
- Judicial mutilation used to eliminate throne rivals is prohibited; perpetrators are treated under anti-usurpation logic.
- A reign is fully constituted only with all four: legal succession/designation, bicameral ratification, patriarchal coronation, and proclamation before army/citizens.
- The law cannot be changed by the emperor alone; Synkletos and Syllogos consent is required.

## Imperial court and administration

### Cabinet / logothesia — ESTABLISHED
The reorganized central government contains a permanent **Megas Logothetes** coordinating nine logothesia:
1. Genikon — finance/treasury.
2. Logothesion ton Nomon — justice/legal review.
3. Logothesion ton Thematon — themes, internal order, population administration.
4. Dromos — diplomacy, envoys, foreign observation.
5. Stratiotikon — military payroll, supply, records, promotion/service review.
6. Demosios Dromos — posts, beacon/communications network.
7. Logothesion ton Agron — state farms, remounts, irrigation, mills, grain supply, agricultural diffusion.
8. Logothesion tou Oikou — imperial household/property and court operation.
9. Logothesion ton Technon — workshops, shipbuilding, fortification, technical development.

### Megas Symboulos / spelling pending — ESTABLISHED institution, TERM PENDING
- Emperor-direct advisory/research body used to translate Alexios's future knowledge into workable policy, staffed with younger practical talent.
- Manuscript alternates **메가스 심볼로스 / 메가스 심불로스**. Do not silently standardize until the open terminology finding is resolved.

### Recruitment and records — ESTABLISHED
- **Epidokimasia**: open, merit-oriented selection path used to recruit officials/judges outside hereditary elite networks; expands from capital/Thrace to major provincial cities.
- Paper, Indian numerals, arithmographia, double-entry bookkeeping, standardized forms, and printed reference material are progressively adopted in state administration.
- The combined bureaucratic format is referred to as **황은 양식** in later chapters.

### Audit — ESTABLISHED
- **Logisterion (감사청)** revived/created as a distinct audit organ by splitting audit functions from Ophthalmos.
- Audits all departments, including the vestiarion/imperial household finances.
- All government procurement contracts receive post-audit comparison.
- Officeholders must close accounts before resignation is accepted.
- Original/copy/summary records are stored separately; auditors rotate through departments.
- Head: **Megas Logariastes ton Sekreton**; appointment requires Synkletos/Syllogos vetting and imperial appointment; reports directly to emperor.

## Judiciary and law

### Courts — ESTABLISHED
- Provincial courts and circuit judges absorb disputes generated by land/tax reform.
- Final court: **Kritai Katholikoi ton Rhomaion**, replacing/consolidating older high courts.
- Nine **Dikaiodotai**; default consensus, vote when consensus is impossible.
- Four imperial appointments; three chosen by emperor from Syllogos-recommended double slate; two confirmed from patriarchal recommendations.
- Head is the **Megas Dikaiodotes** chosen by emperor from the nine.
- Final judgments cannot be normally appealed, but emperor may issue a reasoned remand; after lawful reconsideration the emperor must accept the new judgment.
- Political design intent: channel elite/popular conflict into litigation rather than revolt.

## Church

- Orthodoxy remains foundational to imperial legitimacy and imperial succession.
- The emperor does **not** resolve the East-West schism or Filioque dispute; cooperation with Latins during the crusade is diplomatic/liturgical where possible, not church union.
- Theodore Balsamon becomes Patriarch **Theodore II** during Alexios's absence and remains a major conservative legal-theological counterweight.
- Church institutions can challenge imperial policy through law and canon reasoning; Alexios generally answers by written legal distinction rather than simply silencing them.
- After Jerusalem, Alexios protects Muslim worship at the holy places; this raises tension with Balsamon but the emperor's status as recoverer of the Holy City gives him unusual room.

## Roman identity and subject status — ESTABLISHED by chapters 100–101

- "Roman" is deliberately redefined as an overarching **political** category capable of including ethnic/religious outsiders who are governed by Roman law, pay Roman taxes, receive Roman protection, and owe Roman loyalty.
- Orthodoxy is no longer a necessary condition for the broad political label "Roman."
- The system distinguishes at least three nested/legal-social categories discussed in the manuscript: **성원**, **시민**, **신민**.
- Church authority is preserved over who counts as an ecclesiastical **성원**; non-Orthodox subjects cannot become such members without conversion.
- **시민** and **신민** are treated as secular/legal categories rather than categories the Church can exclusively define.
- This distinction allows Muslim craftsman **유수프 빈 이브라힘 알라디키** (표기 변형 `알라다키` 존재; terminology finding open) to be recognized as Roman and protected/promoted without declaring Islam equivalent to Orthodoxy.
- This remains politically controversial in Constantinople and is an active integration project, not a settled modern equality regime.

## Military

### Command — ESTABLISHED
- Megas Domestikos and Megas Doux remain core high commands; Alexios personally campaigns and often uses emperor-direct elite/combined formations.
- Alexios Branas becomes a central field commander and, by the 1190s, Megas Domestikos.
- Andronikos Kontostephanos is a foundational naval/command figure through the crusade era and retires by 1192; later Megas Doux office is held by Constantine Angelos in the current period.

### Gunpowder system — ESTABLISHED
- State secret workshops under Leon develop gunpowder weapons over multiple years, with repeated fatal failures.
- **Cheirobolon**: matchlock-style shoulder firearm; reliability milestone of 100 consecutive shots on **1187-08-04**.
- **Pyrobolon**: heavy bronze cannon used mainly for siege/breaching; initially expensive, inaccurate, and logistically demanding.
- **Brontes**: specialist corps expanding from mine/demolition work into gunpowder handling, training, and related tasks.
- **Syntagma / "불꽃의 신타그마"**: mixed formation combining firearm troops with conventional close-order protection/support.
- Early deployment is restricted and formula/workforce are compartmentalized under Ophthalmos protection.
- By the post-crusade period, Frederick Barbarossa has obtained enough knowledge to end any assumption of permanent Roman monopoly.

### Fortification — ESTABLISHED
- Alexios sponsors lower, projecting, mutually supporting fortress designs inspired by later bastioned logic, first at strategic fronts such as Dyrrachium.
- Treat as an accelerated alternate-history development, not as literal full mature trace italienne everywhere.

### Military professionalization — MIXED
- ESTABLISHED: payroll/records, merit recruitment, standardized training, combined arms, logistics, state workshops, improved communications.
- PLANNED / NOT YET FULLY ESTABLISHED: academy/staff-college/general-staff style institutions discussed after the crusade.

## Economy and finance

### Tax/land reform — ESTABLISHED
- Simplification and consolidation of proliferated taxes; systematic land survey/re-measurement; stronger central reporting and cross-checking.
- Privileges are treated as conditional grants whose abuse can justify revocation; forged/abusive exemptions are litigated and confiscated under law.
- Reform goal is not merely higher rates but broader compliance, simpler burdens, and less leakage.

### Metron / standards — ESTABLISHED
- **Metron law** creates imperial master standards and increasingly mandatory measures for taxes, public deliveries, official documents, ports/customs, courts, and other state interfaces.
- Private daily usage transitions more gradually.
- Standardized packaging improves port throughput and contributes to Constantinople's growing commercial information advantage.

### State debt and markets — ESTABLISHED
- **Ophelia Basilike** is used as state borrowing for major reconstruction/war needs.
- Later **황은채** becomes a tradable state security; foreign merchants, including Venetians, hold it.
- A licensed public bond market/"stoa" and official pricing/clearing emerge; the state intervenes against destabilizing mass selloffs by buying/burning its own debt when necessary.

### Banking — ESTABLISHED by chapters 102–104
- Tραπεzitai evolve from money-changers/deposit-takers into networked deposit, lending, remittance, bill-of-exchange, and securities businesses aided by paper records and the postal network.
- Largest operators become known as **Megas Trapezitai**; key names: Theodore Argyros, Elias ben Mordecai, Anna Kataplorina, Leon Chrysocheir.
- **Koini Trapeza** is designed as a common/central banking institution for clearing, monetary/credit stability, and crisis liquidity, explicitly to prevent future emperors from casually debasing/manipulating money and to counter banking panics.
- Alexios's stated direction is to separate monetary power from unilateral imperial whim; do not assume modern central-bank independence beyond what is explicitly enacted.

### Agriculture — ESTABLISHED / ongoing
- Three-field rotation becomes widespread enough to produce measurable gains in output, population, remount quality, and famine resilience.
- The crusade regency establishes a state grain buy-in/release stabilization mechanism: buy surplus in good years, release in bad years.
- A four-field system using **medike/alfalfa + turnips/root crops** begins via state/imperial lands and voluntary private adoption with seed, livestock, and tax incentives.
- Registration requirements also improve land/tenant information and intentionally create leverage over mobile pastoralists.
- Expansion of enclosed/registered pasture generates conflict with Turkic transhumance routes.

### Industry — ESTABLISHED
- Paper production, metal movable type/printing, state technical workshops, and spinning wheel diffusion materially increase administrative and textile capacity.
- Yusuf ibn Ibrahim wins a technical competition/improvement process around the spinning wheel and is appointed **Protomaistor ton Basilikon Ergasterion**, becoming a test case for Muslim/Turkic inclusion.

## Intelligence and security

- **Ophthalmos**: emperor-centered security/intelligence apparatus used for counter-subversion, secret-workshop security, protection, investigation, and coercive operations; headed by an Ophthalmarches (Nikephoros in the established period).
- Alexios later regards it as dangerously powerful and deliberately removes audit functions into Logisterion.
- **Skepsis / scout-intelligence service** operates foreign/strategic reporting by the 1190s; Dimitrios Tornikes appears as a senior chief (Skep­tarches) in the current period.

## Education and knowledge

### University reform — ESTABLISHED in legal design, expansion ongoing
- Rebuild/integrate the Pandidakterion and law-school tradition.
- Preparatory **enkyklios paideia** covers trivium/quadrivium, with Indian numerals and arithmographia integrated into arithmetic.
- Higher faculties: law, medicine, theology, philosophy, technology/arts.
- Planned regional specialization/expansion in Thessaloniki, Nicaea, Athens.
- Academic speech/doctrine inside the university receives broad protection except treason and blasphemy.

## Diplomacy and international order

### Sicily — ESTABLISHED
- William II's large invasion is defeated after the Dyrrachium campaign; Roman victory produces reparations, restrictions, prisoners, and strategic breathing room.
- Alexios gains the victory epithet **Normannoktonos** in the manuscript.

### Serbia — ESTABLISHED
- Stefan Nemanja submits after the Norman-war settlement; obligations/hostage arrangements restore Roman leverage rather than immediate direct annexation.

### Holy Roman Empire — ESTABLISHED
- Frederick Barbarossa and Alexios recognize a carefully negotiated "Two Romes" modus vivendi and conduct a joint crusade while remaining rival emperors with separate churches and states.
- Barbarossa survives the historical Saleph disaster.
- Post-crusade relationship is respectful but increasingly competitive; Henry's succession and Western centralization become future strategic risks.

### Rum Sultanate / Anatolia — ESTABLISHED
- Kilij Arslan II does not follow the same historical fragmentation path; he resists the crusading-Roman coalition.
- After Philomelion/Iconium, Kaykhusraw is enthroned as a Roman-dependent ruler.
- Large parts of central/western Anatolia return to direct Roman administration; a reduced dependent Rum polity remains farther east.

### Antioch and Cilician Armenia — ESTABLISHED
- Bohemond III's Antioch is treated/confirmed as under Roman suzerainty, with stronger garrison/succession leverage after the crusade.
- Levon receives a royal title while accepting Roman overlordship; autonomy is substantial but not full independence.

### Jerusalem / Ayyubids — ESTABLISHED
- Acre falls to the coalition; massacre is prohibited.
- After a hard campaign and negotiated settlement, Jerusalem returns to Christian rule under **Conrad I of Montferrat**.
- Muslim residents and worship at the holy places are protected under a medieval confessional legal order, not modern equal citizenship.
- A truce with Saladin includes prisoner/hostage arrangements and territorial/financial concessions; Alexios prefers a stable negotiating counterpart to total Ayyubid destruction.
- After Saladin's death in the current early-1190s political landscape, Roman policy is to observe the succession struggle and consider limited support for al-Adil only if needed for regional stability.

### Italian maritime republics — ESTABLISHED trend
- Venice/Genoa/Pisa continue trading but face reduced information/privilege advantages as Roman customs, standards, finance, and shipping capacity improve.
- Enrico Dandolo becomes Doge in **1192** and begins treating Roman financial-commercial dominance as an existential Venetian problem; he opens a line toward Henry in the western empire.

## Turkic integration — ESTABLISHED / current arc

- Alexios refuses either total expulsion or simple preservation of autonomous nomadic structures.
- Policy uses registration, legal transhumance routes, contracts with four-field farms, imperial protection, and market access to pull cooperating groups into the state.
- Groups that refuse and fight are suppressed with extreme violence; this is deliberate protagonist policy and must remain a moral/political issue.
- Named examples:
  - **Abdar tribe**: Aydogan Bey; son **Tungar**; becomes a registered nomad community.
  - **Bayundur tribe**: led by **Erten Hatun**; enters winter-pasture/livestock contracts while retaining summer migration.
- Cultural integration culminates, for the current arc, in the revival of equestrian contests and the **Ta Basilika Tzykanistika**; Anatolikon meros wins the **Diskos tis Tykhes/Tikis** in spring 1194.

## Technology diffusion rule for future review

- Do not treat a prototype as empire-wide merely because Alexios knows the concept.
- Existing manuscript pattern: secret prototype → workshop iteration/failures → military/administrative pilot → regulated diffusion → second-order social/economic effects.
- Gunpowder remains the most tightly controlled technology; paper, bookkeeping, measures, printing, agriculture, and spinning diffuse more broadly.
