# Character Update — Chapter 118

## Leon Chrysocheir

- One of the Megas Trapezitai and the most aggressive large banker in the empire.
- Nickname: **황금 손**.
- Highly competent and quick to understand Alexios's desired financial modernization.
- Builds a deeply interconnected institution spanning deposits, commercial finance, `황은채`, bill guarantees, maritime lending and trade ventures.
- Uses deposited funds aggressively rather than leaving large idle reserves.
- Guarantees transactions worth several times his own funds.
- Initially reacts rationally to the Saint Mark Panic:
  - honors withdrawals,
  - buys bonds to defend confidence,
  - later sells bonds for liquidity when cash demands become overwhelming.
- His strategic error is confidence that his systemic importance and Koini Trapeza make rescue inevitable.
- By the chapter endpoint he has declared payment suspension but is still described as book-solvent.

## Theodore Argyros

- Appears as a more conservative Megas Trapezites.
- Warns Leon that his increasingly aggressive business and guarantee of other people's bills are dangerous.
- Functions as an internal financial-sector contrast to Leon's risk appetite.

## Enrico Dandolo

- Designs the Saint Mark Panic as a direct financial offensive after the proxy fronts fail to impose enough cost on Rome.
- Understands the moral-hazard implications of Koini Trapeza.
- Knows the attack will likely expose Venetian responsibility and imperil remaining privileges.
- Proceeds because Venice and Henry are paying rising costs to sustain the wider anti-Roman coalition and both face internal political constraints.
- Seeks to force Alexios rapidly toward negotiation rather than simply destroy trade for its own sake.

## Innocent III

- Established in chapter 118 as newly elected in January 1198.
- In this timeline explicitly recognizes Emeric's legitimacy and excommunicates Andrew.
- His stance compounds political pressure on the Henry–Andrew–Venetian side.
