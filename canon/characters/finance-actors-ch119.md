# Finance / Administrative Actor Update — Chapter 119

## Elias ben Mordecai
- Takes operational leadership inside Koini Trapeza when the institution initially panics.
- Orders collection of Chrysocheir clearing and transaction records.
- Becomes a central executor of Alexios's one-day balance-sheet/maturity survey.

## Theodore Argyros
- Initially angry at Leon's role in creating the panic.
- His own institution is also exposed to withdrawal pressure.
- Participates in Koini Trapeza's coordinated response.

## Anna Kataplorina
- Her trapeza also faces deposit withdrawals.
- Initially frightened by the panic but joins the collective Koini Trapeza response.

## Megas Logothetes Choniates
- Restores order inside the finance administration.
- Orders consolidated treasury/revenue/emergency-resource reporting.
- Coordinates reporting with Logisterion, Ophthalmos, Skepsis and Demosios Dromos.
- Initiates emergency parliamentary preparations.

## Leon Chrysocheir
- Accepts emergency resolution under imperial edict.
- Loses management control.
- His equity becomes first-loss capital.
- Personal assets are pledged within guarantee responsibility.
- The viable parts of his network survive in a temporary trapeza while impaired assets are segregated for liquidation.
- Chapter 119 therefore confirms that Leon's expectation of being personally rescued because he is systemically important is false.

## Alexios II
- Directs crisis policy by separating:
  - solvency from liquidity,
  - owner rescue from system rescue,
  - market-price collapse from sovereign repayment capacity,
  - ordinary redemption from large-scale payment sequencing.
- Uses emergency imperial authority for immediate action but voluntarily sends the supplementary fiscal burden to Syllogos for political ratification.
