# Core Cast Addendum — Chapter 115

> This addendum supplements `canon/characters/core-cast.md` with imperial-family facts established in chapter 115.

## Alexios II Komnenos — UPDATED

- By 1195 Alexios's concern for Anna's second delivery has driven empire-level maternal-health reform rather than only palace precautions.
- He remains capable of battlefield composure but experiences acute fear during Anna's labor, explicitly distinguishing the vulnerability of family from military danger.
- After the twins' birth he promises them a world in which they can live safely, reinforcing the dynastic/personal motive behind his institutional project.
- Current immediate family: Anna, eldest son/co-emperor Manuel, younger son Ioannes, younger daughter Maria.

## Anna / Agnes of France — UPDATED

- In **1195** Anna safely gives birth to twins in the purple birth chamber.
- She survives the delivery and is conscious, joking with Alexios afterward.
- The birth confirms her continuing role as both emotional anchor and mother of the enlarged imperial dynasty.

## Manuel Porphyrogennetos — UPDATED

- Present outside the birth chamber with Alexios during Anna's 1195 labor.
- He is portrayed as unusually precocious for his age but still visibly anxious for his mother.
- After the birth he meets his two younger siblings and reacts with childlike surprise at their wrinkled newborn appearance.
- He remains the already-established elder son and co-emperor; chapter 115 does not alter his succession position.

## Ioannes Porphyrogennetos — NEW

- Born in **1195** as one of Anna's twins.
- Male child born in the purple; explicitly hailed as **Porphyrogennetos**.
- Named **Ioannes / 요안니스** after Alexios's grandfather.
- Described as physically resembling Anna despite being a boy.
- No office, succession adjustment or later characterization is yet established beyond birth and name.

## Maria Porphyrogeniti — NEW

- Born in **1195** as one of Anna's twins.
- Female child born in the purple; explicitly hailed as **Porphyrogeniti**.
- Named **Maria / 마리아** after Alexios's mother.
- Described as having inherited Alexios's darker complexion.
- No later political role or betrothal is yet established.
