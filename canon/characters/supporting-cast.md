# Supporting Character Canon

> Supporting-character facts established by canonical manuscripts. Do not promote inferred backstory beyond what the manuscript states.

## Kutlu Arslan

- First canonical appearance: **ch108, `바실리케 켈리티카`**.
- Age in spring 1194: approximately **24**.
- Identity: Turkic Muslim man from the Phrygian Roman–Rum frontier.
- Family/background: born into a small Turkic tribal community; father was a noted horseman but only a middle/lower-status warrior. Separated from his family at age sixteen during inter-tribal conflict and does not expect them to be alive.
- Pre-Roman life: moved among several beys and effectively lived as a mercenary cavalryman.
- Entry into Roman service: fought on the Rum side under a bey during the Roman crusade-era Anatolian campaign, was captured by the Romans, and accepted contract-soldier status.
- Current service: serves as a contract cavalryman with the **Tourkopouloi**; chapter 108 explicitly establishes that he remains Muslim.
- Current loyalty: after roughly four years following Alexios's armies, he has been impressed by the emperor's courage, strategy, and magnanimity and believes service under him gives meaning/stability to an otherwise wandering life. Do not overstate this as total ideological assimilation.
- Temperament: taciturn, self-effacing, difficult to provoke in most matters; unusually sensitive about insults to horsemanship because he treats riding skill as inherited family/tribal identity.
- Equestrian style: understands and feels the horse rather than merely forcing it; uses deliberate pacing and preserves the mount's endurance for the later stages of a race.
- Ch108 result: enters the **Basilike Kelitika** after hearing Latin riders dismiss Turkic mounted warfare as cowardly hit-and-run tactics; wins first place riding **Karagöz** and receives the **Mega Kypellon** directly from Alexios.
- Public status after ch108: the Hippodrome crowd first chants for the "black horse" and then chants **Arslan** by name. This establishes a moment of public cultural incorporation without erasing existing anti-Turkic or anti-Muslim prejudice.

## Karagöz

- First canonical appearance: **ch108**.
- Kutlu Arslan's horse; name is glossed in narration as **"검은 눈"**.
- Appearance: small and rather unimpressive beside large Latin warhorses; black-eyed and black-coated in the manuscript description.
- Temperament: gentle, responsive, expressive with Kutlu.
- Performance profile: not the fastest horse in absolute terms, but intelligent and markedly superior in endurance/stamina; its race advantage emerges as other horses tire.
- Ch108 result: wins the Basilike Kelitika with Kutlu through conservative early pacing and a strong late move; the crowd initially identifies and cheers the pair through the horse as **"검은 말"**.

## Manuel Kontostephanos

- First newly locked details in ch108: son of **Andronikos Kontostephanos**.
- Competes with the **Kontostephanos meros**, which takes third place in the imperial tzykanion event.
- Alexios personally awards him a bronze-colored medal and says that Manuel has again brightened a family name that had been tarnished.
- Chapter 108 locks the existence of a blemish on the family's public reputation but does **not by itself** specify the precise cause; use separate author-approved canon for the cause if needed.

## Theodore Laskaris — ch108 update

- Leads/is the focal figure of the **Laskaris meros**, runner-up to Anatolikon after the 4–3 final established in ch107.
- Ch108 establishes his response to defeat: he recognizes that his side was beaten fairly in skill/preparation and by its own complacency, offers congratulations first, and publicly accepts the result.
- Alexios calls him a rising new star of the reign and praises his attitude after defeat.
