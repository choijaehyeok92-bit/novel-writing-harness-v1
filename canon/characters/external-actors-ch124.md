# External Actors Addendum — Chapter 124

> Source: `manuscripts/reviewed/124.무엇을 남길 것인가 (3).txt`.
> Updates present status only; prior confinement, alliances and frontier stalemates remain part of each character's history.

## Henry VI

- After Bouvines, recognizes the danger of Richard-backed Welf/princely pressure while committing resources eastward.
- Accepts Conrad's proposal for a secret accommodation with Richard and assurances for Edward's inheritance.
- Then confronts deteriorating proxy fronts and a fragile Venetian financing/regime structure.
- Trades legal/property/clerical concessions and Frederick's rehabilitation for princely troops, transport and nonrebellion pledges.
- Retains the source's urgent-intervention reservation; the compact is not absolute renunciation of imperial action.
- Expects eastern conquest and the prestige of both Romes to recover the authority conceded at home.
- Orders direct expedition without prior ultimatum. Actual conquest or border crossing is not yet shown.

## Conrad of Querfurt / 콘라트 폰 케르푸르트

- Architect of both the Richard accommodation and the bargain separating negotiable princes from Henry's uncompromising opponents.
- Recognizes Richard's dynastic priorities and the princes' concerns over land and status.
- Continues as Henry's principal political adviser; no new office is created.

## Richard I and Edward

- Richard seeks to consolidate his gains for Edward rather than pursue indefinite confrontation with Henry.
- Accepts the secret recognition/nonaggression/non-intervention pact in exchange for hereditary and succession assurances and non-support for challengers.
- Remains friendly toward Rome but promises no military intervention in the Two-Romes war.
- Edward's inheritance obtains another assurance. No age/date revision or imperial coronation is established.
- The pact does not demonstrate that every Welf prince follows Richard's orders or that their domestic opposition ends.

## Frederick VI, Duke of Swabia — STATUS CHANGED

- **No longer confined.** Henry confirms no treason, releases him and restores ducal honor, property management and legitimate audience/contact rights.
- Accepts the request to serve in the coming expedition as a senior commander, invoking loyalty as a subject and Hohenstaufen family member.
- No new ambition to overthrow Henry, independent regency, succession entitlement or exact unit command is established.

## Qutb al-Din Malikshah / 쿠트브 앗딘 — DECEASED

- Murdered in his sleep by the emirs who had promoted his claim.
- Head offered to Kaykhusraw as part of their surrender.
- This ends the specific Seljuk-front rebellion described in the chapter.

## Kaykhusraw / 케이휘스레브

- Receives his elder brother's head with bitterness and relief.
- The surrendering emirs expect his Roman-backed legitimacy and limited independent authority to prevent easy reprisals.
- Their expectation is not a narrated irrevocable amnesty or proof all internal threats have disappeared.

## Levon and the Hetumids

- Levon remains tied down by Hetumid opposition pursuing its own power ambitions alongside Roman instigation.
- No completed regime replacement or Levon's death/capture is stated.

## Andrew / 언드라시; Vukan / 부칸

- Western-backed positions deteriorate against Emeric/Stefan and Roman support.
- Andrew's supporting Hungarian lords increasingly hedge.
- Vukan warns of inability to endure even another six months.
- Both remain active and are named as requesting Henry's expedition assistance. Their defeat/death is not yet established.

## Enrico Dandolo

- Still controls Venice, but merchant financiers press him angrily for promised western privileges and eastern-war profits.
- Pro-Dandolo houses furnish much of the support going to Andrew/Vukan; tied-up funds rather than expected returns threaten their support.
- Regime collapse and subsequent pro-Roman realignment remain feared possibilities, not completed events.
