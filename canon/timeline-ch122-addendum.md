# Timeline Addendum — Chapter 122

## 1194

- Richard I and Berengaria of Navarre have a legitimate son, **Edward**.
- Edward becomes Richard's acknowledged heir.
- Richard establishes a succession/regency framework:
  - Berengaria and Eleanor of Aquitaine as guardians,
  - Hubert Walter and William Marshal on the regency council.
- William Marshal is elevated to Earl of Pembroke earlier than in original history.
- Hubert Walter, Archbishop of Canterbury and Justiciar, is empowered to coordinate English government.

## 1194–1199 — cumulative divergence

- Richard avoids the historical post-crusade captivity under Henry VI.
- The historical ransom shock and large cash transfer to Germany do not occur.
- John's historical rebellion/royal usurpation attempt does not develop on the same path.
- Philip II lacks the same opportunity to exploit Richard's captivity against Normandy.
- Crusader settlement customs from Damietta, Haifa and Acre contribute to Richard's revenues.
- Over several years, Richard's gunpowder/firearm program produces royal arms workshops, powder magazines, military accounting, port depots, procurement offices and attempts at ammunition/firearm standardization.

## 1199 — Venice

- Dandolo's chapter-121 seizure becomes an open authoritarian consolidation.
- Councils are intimidated into ratifying temporary special authority.
- Pietro Ziani is confined in a monastery rather than killed.
- Henry VI's German agents/mercenaries are confirmed as participants in the coercive operation.
- Firearms supplied through those Germans are used by Dandolo's armed following against an armed Ziani counter-mobilization.
- Open resistance is suppressed, not eliminated.

## 1199 — Bouvines

- Richard I defeats Philip II of France at **Bouvines**.
- Philip II is captured.
- Exact battle date, tactics, coalition composition, casualties and peace terms remain **OPEN** until later manuscript canon establishes them.

## After the Bouvines victory — declared political direction

- Richard seeks to end French royal overlordship over his continental lands and transmit the cross-Channel inheritance intact to Edward.
- A formal Angevin imperial title or coronation is **not yet established**.
