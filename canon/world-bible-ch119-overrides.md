# World Bible Override — Chapter 119

## Financial crisis architecture — now ESTABLISHED in operation

Chapter 119 converts several previously designed functions into on-page practice.

### Koini Trapeza
- Acts as lender of last resort to designated trapezai.
- Accepts specified collateral subject to substantial haircuts.
- Charges emergency rates above the normal rate.
- Operates central clearing and daily net settlement.
- Can remove a failed participant and admit a temporary successor institution.
- Centralizes large grammation redemption while maintaining ordinary redemption.
- Can apply temporary emergency collateral schedules to state securities.

### Emergency bank resolution
- Imperial emergency edict can place a failed/systemic trapeza into temporary management.
- Owner management rights can be suspended.
- Owner equity is used as first-loss capital.
- Personal assets may be pledged within the owner's guarantee responsibility.
- Sound assets, deposits and payment functions can be transferred to a temporary institution.
- Bad/speculative assets and guarantees can be segregated into a liquidation estate.
- Chapter 119 establishes this as an emergency practice; it does **not** yet establish a permanent codified bank-resolution statute.

### State securities
- `황은채` emergency collateral valuation can differ from panic market price.
- The chapter's explicit example is 70% of face against a market price near 60%.
- Preferential emergency valuation is restricted to pre-panic recorded holdings to block arbitrage.
- It is explicitly temporary/emergency treatment.

### Grammation
- Ordinary/small redemption remains guaranteed during the panic.
- Large commercial redemption is processed centrally and sequenced, not refused.
- Inter-trapeza payments can be netted through Koini Trapeza.
- Small personal deposits, payroll accounts and merchant settlement accounts receive crisis protection.

## Administrative capacity

- Paper records, standardized accounting, Koini Trapeza transaction data and Logisterion staffing allow a one-day simplified balance-sheet/maturity survey of major trapezai.
- Crisis analysis explicitly distinguishes solvency from liquidity and examines maturity structure rather than only aggregate asset value.

## Fiscal / constitutional practice

- Crisis intervention reaches the ceiling of the current finance budget.
- The manuscript states that direct imperial authority is legally sufficient for supplementary action, but Alexios voluntarily submits an emergency supplementary budget to Syllogos.
- This chapter therefore establishes **voluntary imperial use of representative fiscal ratification in crisis**, not a new rule that the emperor legally lacks emergency fiscal power.
- No final appropriation is enacted on-page yet.

## Security / foreign response

- Ophthalmos prepares, but does not yet execute, a proposal for Venetian exit restrictions and searches.
- Skepsis assesses the panic as an organized Venetian financial attack.
- Possible cancellation of Venetian privileges/treaty rights is discussed but not enacted.
